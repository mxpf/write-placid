A note can be small without being unfinished.

It can hold one observation, one useful link, or a sentence that keeps returning. The shape is not a lesser essay. It is simply the amount of room the thought needs.

> A publishing practice becomes easier when every idea does not have to become an event.

Write it clearly. Give it a stable address. Let it remain exactly as large as it wants to be.
